Comprehensive Specifications

Every detail at Shree Vishwa is crafted with branded fittings, durable construction, and thoughtful inclusions to give you an effortless lifestyle from day one.

Structure & Masonry

  • R.C.C.: Earthquake resistance R.C.C. frame structure.
  • Brick work: External wall 6" thick and internal wall 4.5" thick brick work.
  • Structural longevity: Engineered to seismic safety standards by Er. Prasanna Bhore.

Plaster & Paint

  • Double coat external plaster with weather-resistant semi Acrylic paint.
  • Neeru finish internal plaster throughout the entire flat.
  • ASIAN Oil Bound Distemper paint applied on all interior walls.

Flooring & Kitchen

  • Good quality 2' × 2' Vitrified (Johnson or equivalent) flooring for entire flat.
  • Granite L-shape kitchen platform or Granite kitchen & service platform (G20).
  • Heavy-gauge stainless steel sink and full-height glazed tile dado.

Doors & Windows

  • 3 track powder coated aluminium sliding windows with Mosquito Net.
  • Protective M. S. safety grill & elegant polished marble frames.
  • Laminated main door with polished brass fittings and high-security night latch.
  • Waterproof flush doors with both-side laminate for all interior rooms.

Bath & Sanitary

  • Full height glazed ceramic tiles in Bathroom & Toilet.
  • Laminate door shutter with polished Granite frame.
  • Concealed plumbing with good quality branded C.P. fittings & wall mixer.

Electrification & Utilities

  • Concealed copper electrification with modular switches for entire flat.
  • Provision of Telephone cable & Cable TV connection in living room.
  • Separate allocated parking space for each flat.
  • 24 hours uninterrupted water supply of Municipal or boring water.
