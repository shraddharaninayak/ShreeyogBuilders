Shree Bramhand

Bodhle Nagar, Nashik

2 & 3 BHK Luxurious Residences

Architectural Overview

Shree Bramhand stands as a premier residential landmark in Bodhle Nagar, Nashik. Featuring expansive 2 and 3 BHK luxury residences, the project blends architectural sophistication with generous natural ventilation, premium terrace leisure decks, and state-of-the-art security.

A signature residential address thoughtfully planned for discerning families seeking expansive open skies, high-speed vertical mobility, and an elite community atmosphere.

Lifestyle Amenities

  • ✦ Exclusive Terrace Leisure Deck
  • ✦ Dedicated Yoga Space & Open Gym
  • ✦ Children's Play Zone & Gazebo
  • ✦ Acoustic Performance Stage & Party Lawn
  • ✦ Automatic High-Speed Elevators with Power Backup
  • ✦ CCTV Surveillance & Gated 24/7 Security

Key Specifications

Structure

Earthquake resistant RCC framed structure engineered to high safety standards.

Flooring

Premium vitrified tiles in all living, dining, and bedroom areas with anti-skid bathroom ceramic finishes.

Kitchen

Polished granite platform with stainless steel sink and glazed wall tiles up to lintel height.

Doors & Windows

Laminated decorative main entrance door; powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito nets.

Electrical

Concealed copper wiring with modular switches, AC points in master bedroom, and inverter provision.
