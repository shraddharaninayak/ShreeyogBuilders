Business Infrastructure

Designed for Contemporary Commerce

A signature commercial landmark offering high-speed vertical connectivity, dedicated private terraces, and flexible executive suites tailored for professionals, consultants, and enterprises.

High-Speed Automatic Elevator

State-of-the-art 1.80m x 1.80m passenger elevator connecting basement, ground, and all four commercial floors with battery backup.

Dual Level Parking & Ramp

Expansive basement and ground-level demarcated parking bays with a dedicated vehicle ramp, guaranteeing parking ease for staff and visitors.

Executive Attached Toilets

Every office suite includes private 5'0"x7'0" attached toilet facilities with concealed plumbing and premium marble door frames.

Private Executive Terraces

Select suites on upper floors boast expansive attached open terraces (up to 27'0"x6'0") offering scenic skyline views and fresh-air break-out areas.

Wide Circulation Lobbies

Generous 5'4" to 6'0" wide bright entrance foyers, corridors, and staircase landings engineered for smooth professional foot traffic.

Concealed Electrification

High-grade copper wiring with modular switches, internet and telephone conduit provisions, and commercial distribution boards.
