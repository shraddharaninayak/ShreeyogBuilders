Shree Malhar

"The Rhythm of the Delighted Life"

Nestled near the scenic Gangapur Road extension in Savarkar Nagar behind Vishwas Bank, Shree Malhar is a boutique residential landmark offering exclusive 3 BHK luxurious flats. Designed with only two residences per floor, each home enjoys triple-sided openness, four private balconies, dedicated 2-car covered parking bays, and a curated rooftop leisure deck.

Since 1992, Shreeyog Builders & Developers (B. M. Kale) has been shaping property development in Nashik, guided by Quality Construction, Transparent Deals, Customer Satisfaction, and Social Awareness.

The Shreeyog Philosophy

The SHREEYOG BUILDERS & DEVELOPERS was formed with a vision to change the face of property development in Nashik. The firm has built a solid reputation with rock-solid experience spanning more than 30 years in building luxurious residential & commercial projects across Mumbai Naka, Bhabha Nagar, Gangapur Road, Govind Nagar, Indira Nagar, Deepali Nagar, Kamathwada, Bodhle Nagar, Karmyogi Nagar, and Rajiv Nagar.

Meticulous planning, superior design, modern amenities, excellence in quality, and timely completion have made Shreeyog synonymous with reliability and enduring value.
