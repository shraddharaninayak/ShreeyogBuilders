Rigorous Standards • IS Compliant

Technical & Material Specifications

Every structural and architectural element at Shree Nakshatra conforms strictly to Indian Standard (IS) codes, utilizing certified branded materials.

R.C.C. Structure

Earthquake-resistant R.C.C. frame structure engineered strictly as per IS standards with robust column-and-beam grid.

Brick Work & Masonry

External walls 6" thick and internal partition walls 4.5" thick brickwork providing superior thermal and acoustic insulation.

Plaster & Paint

Double-coat external plaster with weather-shield semi-acrylic paint; Neeru-finish internal plaster with Asian Oil Bound Distemper paint.

Flooring

Good quality 2' x 2' Vitrified (Johnson) tile flooring throughout the entire flat with matching skirting.

Kitchen

Polished Granite L-Shape kitchen platform (G20) & stainless steel sink with full-height glazed tile dado.

Windows

3-track powder-coated aluminium sliding windows with stainless steel Mosquito Net, M.S. safety grill & marble sill frame.

Doors

Main door with brass fittings, Night latch and Safety door. All internal doors & frames finished with elegant laminate.

Bath / Toilet

Full-height designer glazed tiles in bathroom & toilet; waterproof PVC doors with marble frame; concealed plumbing with wall mixer.

Electrification

Concealed electrification for entire flat with high-grade copper wiring, modular switches, and adequate light/fan points.

Parking

Demarcated covered car parking space on ground stilt floor allocated to each residential apartment.

Water Supply

24 hours water supply with dual connectivity from Municipal corporation lines and on-site borewell with storage tanks.

Media & Comms

Pre-installed television cable and telephone connection lines in living room for turnkey entertainment setup.
