Shree Navashakti

"Peaceful living for infinity recognition of your divine soul.."

Directly situated at Navshakti Chowk, opposite the lush Dr. Homi Bhabha Garden in Bhabha Nagar, Nashik, Shree Navashakti is an exclusive low-density residential landmark crafted by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers. Comprising 16 private 2 & 3 BHK residences across G + 4 floors (only 4 homes per floor) on a generous 9.00-meter road, Shree Navashakti embodies pure serenity and structural permanence. Featuring natural red soil brick masonry, zero common light bill rooftop solar net-metering, solar water heating for each toilet, a rooftop celebration terrace, and a comprehensive move-in readiness kit, it offers an elevated way of life where every homecoming feels like a spiritual sanctuary.

Legacy • 27+ Years of Trust • Since 1992

Rooted in Four Pillars of Construction Excellence

For over 27 years, Shreeyog Builders & Developers has transformed residential living across Mumbai Naka, Bhabha Nagar, Gangapur Road, Govind Nagar, Indira Nagar, Deepali Nagar, Kamathwada, Bodhale Nagar, Rane Nagar, and Rajiv Nagar.

'SHREE NAVASHAKTI' was envisioned for families seeking genuine serenity without compromising on urban accessibility. At Shree Navashakti, every residence is built upon the four steadfast pillars that define Shreeyog's emblem: Quality Construction, Transparent Deals, Customer's Satisfaction, and Social Awareness.

Unlike contemporary mass developments built with cheap cinder or hollow cement blocks, Shree Navashakti utilizes authentic natural red soil bricks. This time-honoured technique ensures natural climate thermal insulation, cooler room temperatures during peak summer, enhanced acoustic buffering between homes, and higher wall-load stability.

With only 4 residences per floor facing east and west, every flat enjoys uninterrupted sunlight and refreshing garden breezes from Dr. Homi Bhabha Garden right across the street. Shree Navashakti is not just an address; it is a sacred space designed for peace of mind, family togetherness, and lifelong contentment.

Thoughtful Privileges & Move-In Kit

Every detail at Shree Navashakti is curated to eliminate move-in stress and ongoing utility burdens, from zero common light bills to ready-to-use domestic fixtures.

☀️

Zero Common Light Bill

Rooftop solar power net-metering system powering all common passages, parking lot lights, water pump, and elevator at zero recurring electricity cost.

🚿

Solar Water Heating System

Dedicated solar water pipeline providing energy-efficient hot water directly to each bathroom toilet to lower household electrical utility costs.

🎉

Rooftop Party Celebration Terrace

Expansive open-air rooftop celebration terrace fitted with a washbasin and pressurized water supply, tailored for intimate family functions and festivals.

🎁

Turnkey Move-In Gift Kit

Each flat arrives pre-equipped with tube lights, bulbs, door mats, fan, Aqua guard purifier, cloth dryer stand, curtain rods with curtains, letter box, and nameplate.

🍳

Granite Platform & Kitchen Trolley

Polished L-shape or parallel granite kitchen platform with full ceramic tile dado and built-in stainless steel modular kitchen trolley.

🛗

4-Person Automatic Lift

High-speed automatic 4-passenger elevator equipped with emergency battery backup to ensure uninterrupted vertical travel during power interruptions.

📹

CCTV Security & MS Gate

Surveillance CCTV cameras in parking area, CD gate for entrance lobby, heavy-duty MS security gate for main entrance, and on-site watchman room with toilet.

🏀

Basketball Ring & Sports

Dedicated campus basketball hoop, common Tata Sky connectivity, and demarcated stilt covered parking bays for all residents on a 9.00m wide road.

Quality Standards • Enduring Materials

Architectural Specifications

Engineered with natural red soil bricks, Johnson / RAK brand tiles, granite frames, and IS-compliant CPVC plumbing for multi-generational permanence.

🧱

Natural Red Soil Brickwork

External and internal masonry constructed with authentic natural red soil bricks (or eco-light block), delivering superior natural climate thermal insulation and acoustics.

✨

Johnson / RAK Brand Tiles

Premium flooring and wall tiles exclusively from Johnson or RAK brand across all living, dining, bedroom suites, and private sit-out balconies with matching skirting.

🍳

Granite Platform & Trolley

L-shape or parallel polished granite kitchen platform with full ceramic tile dado up to ceiling, stainless steel sink, and built-in modular kitchen trolley.

🚪

Laminated Doors & MS Gate

Box-type plywood door frames with laminate on both sides. All bedroom and main door shutters laminated on both sides. Heavy-duty MS safety gate for main door and CD gate for lobby.

🪟

Granite Windows & Frames

All windows and toilets constructed with durable granite sill frames. Powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with protective safety grills.

🚿

Plumbing & Solar Bathrooms

Full ceramic tiles in each toilet with granite door frames. Dedicated solar water heating line, ISI-marked PVC plumbing material, washbasin with mirror, and bath accessories.

☀️

Rooftop Solar Generation

Rooftop solar electrical power generation grid connected to net-metering, ensuring zero recurring electricity bills for common campus circulation and pumping.

🎨

Paint & Finishes

Internal walls finished in smooth tractor emulsion plastic paint in pleasing aesthetic tones. External facades coated in weather-shield exterior plastic paint.

🏛️

R.C.C. Frame Design

Seismic-resistant reinforced cement concrete frame design engineered strictly in accordance with Bureau of Indian Standards (IS Code) by Er. Bhavesh Patel.
