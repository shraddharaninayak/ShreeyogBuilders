Comprising twin wings (A & B) rising over a dedicated stilt parking level, this distinguished community offers 26 residences including exclusive 4th-floor penthouses with private open sky terraces, landscaped gardens, green gym, and children's play arena.

Architectural Philosophy

Harmonious Living, Divine Comfort

Every square foot at Shree Samarth was planned with spiritual balance, structural integrity, and generous open spaces to nurture multi-generational happiness.

01

Vastu & Natural Light

East-West cross ventilation ensures every room receives invigorating daylight and fresh cross breeze across all floors.

02

Twin Wing Privacy

Independent cores with separate lifts and staircases for Wing A and Wing B maximize privacy and eliminate corridor crowding.

03

Sky Terraces

Exclusive 4th-floor penthouses (A-13 and B-13) feature private rooftop terraces offering panoramic skyline views of Indira Nagar.

04

Community Wellness

Dedicated children's play arena, open-air green gym, performance stage, and lush lawn create a vibrant neighborhood oasis.

Curated Amenities

Quality Engineering

Construction Specifications

Engineered to strict Indian Standard (IS) codes, incorporating premium materials, reliable fittings, and durable craftsmanship throughout.

Structure & Masonry

  • Earthquake-resistant RCC framed structure compliant with IS codes
  • 6" external brickwork with double-coat sand-faced plaster
  • Smooth neeru plaster finish on all interior walls

Flooring & Tiling

  • Premium 2' x 2' vitrified tile flooring in living, dining, and bedrooms
  • Anti-skid designer ceramic tile flooring in terraces & dry balconies
  • Full height glazed ceramic tiles in toilets and bathrooms

Doors & Windows

  • Decorative main door with brass hardware, night latch & nameplate
  • Waterproof flush doors with cylindrical locks for bedrooms
  • Powder-coated 3-track aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh & safety MS grills

Kitchen & Utility

  • Fine-polished granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink
  • Glazed designer tiles up to lintel level above platform
  • Provision for water purifier and exhaust fan

Electrical & Telecom

  • Concealed fire-resistant copper wiring with modular switches
  • Adequate light, fan, TV, and telephone points in living & master bedroom
  • AC point provision in master bedroom & inverter wiring point

Plumbing & Paint

  • Concealed CPVC plumbing with branded CP fittings & sanitaryware
  • Solar water heating provision and overhead/underground water storage tanks
  • Interior premium acrylic wash paint; exterior weather-shield apex paint
