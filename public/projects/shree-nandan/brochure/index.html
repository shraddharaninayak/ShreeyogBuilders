Complete Specifications & Furnished Inclusions

Every apartment at Shree Nandan is delivered fully equipped with premium branded materials, turnkey convenience fixtures, and dedicated lifestyle inclusions.

🏛️

Civil, Masonry & Structure

  • Earthquake-resistant RCC framed structure designed by Er. Bhavesh Patel.
  • Brickwork constructed in natural red soil bricks or premium Eco light blocks.
  • Internal plaster with smooth Gypsum finish; external sand-faced weather plaster.
  • Decorative entrance lobby with stainless steel security gate.
🍳

Kitchen & Modular Trolleys

  • L-shape or parallel premium black granite kitchen platform with full-height dado tiles.
  • Complete SS make modular kitchen trolleys with specialized crockery storage unit.
  • Aquaguard RO water purifier installed and ready in every kitchen.
  • Stainless steel sink with Jaquar long-body bib cock.
  • Dedicated dry balcony / utility with washing machine electrical & plumbing provisions.
🚪

Doors, Windows & Safety

  • Both-side premium laminate finish on all bedroom and entrance door shutters.
  • High-grade heavy-duty M.S. security safety gate on the main entrance door.
  • Polished granite framing for all window sills and toilet door jambs.
  • Anodized powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh.
  • Curtain rods with complete curtains provided for every window in the home.
🚿

Flooring, Bathrooms & Plumbing

  • 800 × 800 mm premium vitrified tile flooring of well-known brand throughout flat.
  • Anti-skid ceramic tiles in all attached toilets and sit-out balconies.
  • Full-height designer ceramic wall dado tiles in every bathroom.
  • ISI marked PVC drainage and CPVC concealed plumbing pipes.
  • Branded Jaquar CP fittings with hot & cold mixer units.
  • Individual geyser heating system installed in every toilet.
  • Designer mirrors above washbasin area and complete bathroom accessories kit.
⚡

Electrical, Solar & Tech

  • Rooftop Solar Net Metering for common lighting — zero common electricity bills.
  • Automatic 6-person elevator with generator (genset) power backup.
  • Concealed copper wiring with modular switches and MCB circuit breakers.
  • Provision for inverter wiring across the entire home.
  • Concealed AC conduit provision in master bedrooms.
  • Central Tata Sky common DTH dish infrastructure.
  • 24/7 CCTV surveillance cameras covering entry, lobby, and parking zones.
🎁

Turnkey Inclusions & Extras

  • Entire flat enlightened with complete tube light sets, energy-efficient bulbs, and ceiling fans.
  • Decorative designer false ceiling crafted in the living room hall.
  • Custom stainless steel shoe rack provided for every flat.
  • Foldable stainless steel clothes drying stand for each home.
