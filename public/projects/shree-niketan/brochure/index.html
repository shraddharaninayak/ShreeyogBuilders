Shree Niketan

"A Peaceful Nest... Nestled Majestically" • 2 BHK Luxury Homes

Standing proudly along a wide 24.00-meter municipal DP road at Gulmohar Colony on DGP Nagar Road in Kamatwada, Nashik, Shree Niketan is an accomplished twin-wing residential and retail community developed by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers. Comprising Stilt + 6 storeys across Wing 'A' and Wing 'B', the development offers 36 luxurious 2 BHK family residences (890 sq.ft saleable) alongside 6 prime ground-floor commercial shops, dedicated parking, dual passenger elevators, internal gas pipelines, and continuous dual water supply.

BUILD INTEGRITY

Technical Specifications

Exhaustive engineering and architectural specifications executed to certified durability standards.

◆ Structure
Earthquake resistant R.C.C. frame structure designed to high seismic safety codes.
◆ Brick Work
External perimeter walls 6" thick and internal partition walls 4.5" thick brick masonry.
◆ Plaster & Paint
Double coat external plaster with semi-acrylic paint; Neeru-finish internal plaster with Asian Oil Bound Distemper.
◆ Flooring
Good quality 2' x 2' Johnson Vitrified tile flooring uniformly laid across the entire flat.
◆ Kitchen Platform
Polished G20 granite platform with stainless steel sink and 4' high designer glazed tile dado.
◆ Aluminium Windows
3-track powder-coated aluminium sliding windows with mosquito netting, safety M.S. grills & marble sills.
◆ Doors & Frames
Laminated entrance main door with brass hardware and Night Latch; waterproof flush internal doors.
◆ Bath & Toilet
7' height glazed wall ceramic tiles; PVC doors with marble frames; concealed plumbing with wall mixer.
◆ Gas Pipeline
Dedicated internal gas pipeline safely routed from the kitchen counter to all bathrooms and toilets.
◆ Electrification
Concealed copper wiring with adequate electrical points, TV socket, and telephone connection in living room.
◆ Parking
Separate allotted covered stilt parking space demarcated for each individual residence.
◆ Water Supply
24-hour dual water supply from Nashik Municipal Corporation mains and reliable deep borewell system.
