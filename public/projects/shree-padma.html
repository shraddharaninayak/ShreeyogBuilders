Architectural Overview

"A place where creates the Fragrance of Happiness..." Conceived by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers, Shree Padma is situated directly opposite Cambridge School in prime Indira Nagar, Nashik.

Planned with multi-wing architecture across Wings A, B, and C, each home offers cross-ventilation, expansive sit-outs, demarcated stilt covered car parking, rooftop solar water heating, and manicured green perimeter gardens.

Lifestyle Amenities

  • ✦ Grand Entrance Gate with Security Cabin & Intercom
  • ✦ Demarcated Stilt Covered Parking Bays for Wings A, B & C
  • ✦ High-Speed Automatic Elevators with Battery Backup
  • ✦ Rooftop Solar Water Heating System
  • ✦ Paved Campus Perimeter with Senior Citizen Sit-Outs
  • ✦ Rainwater Harvesting System & Groundwater Recharge

Key Specifications

Structure

Earthquake-resistant R.C.C. frame structure designed as per IS standards.

Masonry & Plaster

Quality brickwork (6" external & 4" internal) with double-coat sand-faced plaster & gypsum finish.

Flooring

2' x 2' vitrified tiles across living, dining, and bedrooms; anti-skid tiles in balconies.

Kitchen

Granite platform with stainless steel sink, glazed tile dado up to lintel, and exhaust provision.

Windows

3-track powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh & safety MS grills.

Electrification

Concealed copper wiring with modular switches, AC point in master bedroom, and TV points.
