Legacy • Craftsmanship • Since 1992

Built with Natural Red Soil Bricks & Decades of Trust

For over 27 years, Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers has built an unshakeable reputation across Mumbai Naka, Gangapur Road, Govindnagar, Indiranagar, Deepali Nagar, Kamathwada, Bodhale Nagar, and Ranenagar.

'SHREE NIWAS' was created for families who want a home unlike any other and a refined lifestyle to match their attitude. At Shree Niwas, modern conveniences are in abundance, but what sets it apart is the genuine commitment to construction longevity.

Unlike contemporary mass projects built with fly-ash or hollow cement blocks, Shree Niwas employs authentic natural red soil bricks for internal and external masonry. This time-honoured technique ensures natural climate insulation, superior sound dampening, higher wall load capacity, and a cooler ambient temperature during peak summer months.

A flourishing community is one where residents come together to socialize, relax, and enjoy each other's company. Shree Niwas has been consciously designed as a special sanctuary where these connections are encouraged and cultivated — because family and friends are the true pillars upon which life at Shree Niwas rests.

Precision Engineering • Move-In Package

Comprehensive Technical Specifications

Every material and fitting selected for Shree Niwas meets the highest standards of durability, aesthetics, and structural reliability.

🏗️

Structure & Masonry

  • • Earthquake-resistant R.C.C. framed structure designed strictly to IS standards.
  • • Brickwork executed in Natural Red Soil Bricks (or eco light blocks) for superior thermal comfort and solid wall strength.
  • • Double coat sand face external plaster with weather-shield exterior paint.
  • • Internal walls finished with smooth gypsum plaster ready for Tractor emulsion plastic paint.
  • • Staircase, landing & lift area excluded from built-up calculations for honest carpet area.
✨

Flooring & Wall Tiling

  • • Premium flooring and wall tiles of Johnson brand or RAK across all rooms.
  • • Anti-skid ceramic tiles in all toilets, sit-out balconies, and utility wash areas.
  • • Each toilet finished with full-height designer wall tiles up to ceiling level.
  • • Full-height glazed ceramic tile dado above the kitchen platform.
🍳

Kitchen & Storage

  • • L-shape or parallel premium Black Granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink.
  • • Modular kitchen trolley set included with stainless steel wire baskets.
  • • Separate utility wash balcony with washing machine inlet and drainage point.
  • • Pre-installed Aqua Guard water purifier and exhaust fan provision.
🚪

Doors & Windows

  • • Decorative main entrance door with additional safety door & high quality M.S. security gate.
  • • Door frames of box type (in plywood) with both side laminate for all bedroom and main doors.
  • • All toilets and windows finished with complete 4-side granite frames.
  • • Powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito net and safety grills.
  • • Collapsible / CD gate for the ground floor entrance lobby.
🚿

Plumbing & Sanitary

  • • Genuine ISI mark PVC / CPVC concealed plumbing material throughout.
  • • Hot and cold water mixer unit in all bathrooms with shower head.
  • • Solar water connection piped directly to each toilet.
  • • Toilet washbasin area with mirror arrangement and complete bathroom accessories.
⚡

Electrification & Power

  • • Concealed copper wiring with MCB protection and branded modular switches.
  • • Adequate light, fan, TV, and telephone points; AC point provision in master bedroom.
  • • Automatic 4-person elevator with battery backup system for power cuts.
  • • Solar lighting system on roof ensuring zero common light bills.
  • • 24/7 CCTV surveillance in parking and security cabin with toilet.
