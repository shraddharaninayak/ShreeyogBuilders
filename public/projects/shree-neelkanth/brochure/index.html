Curated Amenities & Leisure

Every residence at Shree Neelkanth is complemented by health, recreational, and security amenities designed to enrich your family's daily lifestyle.

Ground Floor Fitness Centre

Dedicated 10'0" x 14'8" indoor fitness centre on the ground floor with private washroom facilities for daily workouts.

Rooftop Yoga & Green Gym

Expansive terrace leisure deck featuring open-air yoga space, green gym equipment, stage, and perimeter sitting benches.

Net Metering (Zero Light Bill)

Solar net-metering provision for common area lighting, eliminating recurring common electricity maintenance costs.

Two High-Speed Elevators

Dual automatic passenger lifts (6'3"x8'0" & 6'3"x6'9") serving all six residential storeys with battery backup power.

Dedicated Demarcated Parking

Covered ground floor four-wheeler and two-wheeler parking allotted separately for every home with a 9.0m wide access road.

Security Cabin & Watchman Room

On-site watchman accommodation (8'3"x12'8") and main entrance security cabin (5'0"x5'0") with M.S. security main gate.

Doors, Windows & Masonry

  • •Brickwork done in natural red soil brick or Eco light blocks.
  • •All bedroom and main door shutters laminated on both sides.
  • •All toilets and windows fitted with full granite frame sills.
  • •Good quality M.S. security gate for main entrance door.
  • •Anodised aluminum sliding windows with safety mosquito nets.

Kitchen, Bathrooms & Flooring

  • •L-shape or parallel granite kitchen platform with full-height dado.
  • •Flooring: 800x800 vitrified tiles of reputed brand throughout.
  • •Each toilet equipped with full-height designer ceramic dado.
  • •ISI mark PVC plumbing materials and Jaquar designer fittings.
  • •Geyser system provided in every toilet.
