Complete Technical Specifications

Over 25 carefully specified construction and lifestyle elements verified directly from page 2 of the official Shree Pandurang brochure.

🏢

Structure & Masonry

  • • Earthquake-resistant RCC frame structure engineered to latest seismic codes.
  • • 6" external red brick / light block walls for superior thermal insulation.
  • • 4" internal partition walls with smooth gypsum plaster finish.
  • • External double-coat sand-faced plaster with premium weather-shield paint.
🚪

Doors & Windows

  • • Decorative main door with premium laminate, night latch, and designer handle.
  • • Waterproof flush internal doors with laminate finish and cylindrical locks.
  • • 3-track powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito net.
  • • Natural granite sill for all windows with protective safety grills.
▦

Flooring & Tiling

  • • 32" × 32" premium double-charged vitrified tiles across entire apartment.
  • • Anti-skid ceramic floor tiles in attached terraces, balconies, and dry yard.
  • • Designer ceramic glazed tiles up to lintel level in all bathrooms.
  • • Polished natural granite / marble for entrance thresholds.
🍳

Kitchen & Bathrooms

  • • Granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink (Nirali / equivalent).
  • • Glazed dado tiles up to lintel level above kitchen cooking counter.
  • • Branded CP bath fittings with hot & cold diverter / mixer units (Jaguar equivalent).
  • • Premium wall-hung or floor-mounted sanitaryware with dual-flush cisterns.
⚡

Electrification & Power

  • • Concealed copper wiring (Polycab / Finolex) with modular switches (Legrand).
  • • AC electrical point provisions in master bedroom.
  • • Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB) & ELCB for ultimate electrical safety.
  • • Solar Net Metering grid system powering all common-area lighting.
🌿

Eco & Safety Inclusions

  • • Automatic 6-passenger elevator with battery backup system.
  • • Rainwater harvesting network with underground recharge pits.
  • • Comprehensive CCTV coverage for lobby, parking, and periphery.
  • • Dedicated demarcated parking bay allocated per apartment.
