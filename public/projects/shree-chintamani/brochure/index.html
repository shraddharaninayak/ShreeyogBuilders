Completed Landmark 2 & 3 BHK Apartments Gangapur Road, Nashik

Shree Chintamani

"The Prestigious Location for Your Home Fulfilled with Happiness.. Peace of Mind.. & Prosperity !"

Situated at Datta Chowk, opposite Mauli Hotel and behind Siddhi Vinayak Apartments on Nirmala Convent Road in prime Gangapur Road, Nashik, Shree Chintamani (श्री चिंतामणी) is an exclusive completed residential community crafted by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers. Rising gracefully across Stilt + 5 floors, Shree Chintamani features 18 luxurious 2 and 3 BHK family residences designed with Vastu compliance, allocated stilt parking, automatic passenger elevator, panoramic vistas of the Godavari River and Godapark, and 24-hour dual municipal and borewell water supply.

CONSTRUCTION RIGOUR

Technical Specifications

Built with uncompromising structural materials and proven architectural craftsmanship.

◆ Structure
Earthquake resistant R.C.C. frame structure designed to rigorous seismic safety standards.
◆ Brick Work
External perimeter walls 6" thick and internal partition walls 4.5" thick brick masonry.
◆ Plaster & Paint
Double coat external plaster with semi-acrylic paint; Neeru-finish internal plaster with Asian Oil Bound Distemper.
◆ Flooring
Good quality 2' x 2' Johnson Vitrified tile flooring uniformly laid across the entire flat.
◆ Kitchen Platform
Granite kitchen platform (G20) with stainless steel sink and 4' high glazed ceramic tile dado.
◆ Aluminium Windows
3-track powder-coated aluminium sliding windows with mosquito netting, safety M.S. grills & marble sills.
◆ Doors & Frames
Laminated entrance main door with brass hardware and Night Latch; waterproof flush internal doors.
◆ Bath & Toilet
7' height glazed wall ceramic tiles; PVC doors with marble frames; concealed plumbing with quality fittings & wall mixer.
◆ Electrification
Concealed copper wiring with adequate electrical points, TV socket, and telephone connection in living room.
◆ Parking
Separate allocated parking space demarcated for each individual residence on ground stilt level.
◆ Water Supply
24-hour dual water supply from Nashik Municipal Corporation mains and reliable deep borewell system.
◆ Vertical Transit
Modern automatic passenger elevator servicing all residential floors with safety battery backup.
