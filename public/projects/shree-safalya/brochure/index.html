Craftsmanship & Engineering

Technical Specifications

Engineered with uncompromising precision, premium materials, and branded fixtures to deliver generations of peace of mind.

Doors & Windows

  • •All door shutters of solid core flush doors with waterproof decorative laminate on both sides.
  • •Heavy-duty powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh.
  • •Full granite sill frames around all window apertures.
  • •Good quality MS designer safety window grills.
  • •Branded brass / SS designer hardware fittings with night latch on main door.

Kitchen & Utility

  • •Premium granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink.
  • •Full-height designer glazed ceramic dado tiles up to beam level.
  • •Modular kitchen trolley setup with dedicated crockery unit.
  • •Dedicated utility dry balcony with washing machine electric & water plumbing.
  • •Garbage chute connection on every floor for hygienic waste disposal.

Bathrooms & Plumbing

  • •Full granite frames for all bathroom and toilet doors.
  • •Full-height anti-skid floor tiles and premium wall tiles in all bathrooms.
  • •ISI marked CP fittings and PVC / CPVC concealed plumbing pipes.
  • •Hot & cold water mixer unit with shower in all bathrooms.
  • •Cloth dryer and designer mirror with full accessory set provided in each toilet.

Electrical & Solar

  • •Concealed fire-resistant copper wiring with branded modular switches.
  • •Inverter wiring provision across all rooms of the residence.
  • •AC copper conduit piping provisions pre-installed in living and all bedrooms.
  • •Solar water heating provision connected to master bathrooms.
  • •Net metering solar system for common area lighting (zero common electricity bill).

Structure & Finishes

  • •Earthquake-resistant RCC frame structure with Eco-light AAC block masonry.
  • •Internal walls finished with smooth gypsum / POP plaster and luxury emulsion paint.
  • •External walls coated with weather-proof Asian Apex / textured paint.
  • •Large-format vitrified flooring tiles throughout living, dining, and bedrooms.
  • •Staircase, landing, and lift area not considered in carpet calculation.

Elevators & Security

  • •Two automatic 14 to 16 passenger stretcher-sized elevators with battery backup (ARD).
  • •24/7 CCTV surveillance across entrance gate, lobbies, parking, and perimeter.
  • •Decorative grand security entrance gate with guard cabin and common washroom.
  • •Dedicated 2 covered car parking slots per residence with wide driveways.
  • •Rooftop terrace party basin & water arrangement for family functions.
