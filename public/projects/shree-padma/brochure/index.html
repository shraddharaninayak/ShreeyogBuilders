Shree Padma

"A place where creates the Fragrance of Happiness..."

Located right opposite the prestigious Cambridge School in the heart of Indira Nagar, Nashik, Shree Padma is an architectural milestone by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers. Conceived as a serene sanctuary where every human connection flourishes, Shree Padma offers elegantly proportioned 2 & 3 BHK premium residences across Wings A, B, and C. With ground stilt covered parking, manicured garden open spaces, modern elevators, solar water heating, and unmatched connectivity to Nashik's key educational, healthcare, and retail avenues, life here blossoms with lasting joy.

Legacy • Family Values • Since 1992

Cherish Your Human Connections

A home is not merely concrete and steel; it is the sacred space where relationships thrive, laughter echoes, and generations grow together.

'SHREE PADMA' has been planned with the understanding that friends and family are the true pillars of a fulfilling life. By bringing together thoughtfully designed living spaces with warm community corners, Shree Padma cultivates an environment where neighbours become lifelong friends and families experience a true sense of belonging.

With over 27 years of building excellence (Since 1992), Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers has earned an unblemished reputation across Nashik's finest locales including Mumbai Naka, Gangapur Road, Govind Nagar, Indira Nagar, and Ranenagar. At Shree Padma, this legacy is reflected in every detail — from the robust earthquake-resistant RCC structure to the smooth, sunlit rooms oriented for ideal ventilation.

The distinctive multi-wing layout (Wings A, B, and C) provides residents with maximum privacy and cross ventilation, avoiding the claustrophobia of typical dense apartments. Every home features spacious living rooms with sit-out balconies, well-ventilated kitchens with dedicated utility spaces, and serene bedroom suites.

Thoughtful Campus Privileges

Every amenity at Shree Padma has been curated to simplify daily living, provide security, and create pleasant recreational spaces for every family member.

Grand Entrance Gate & Cabin

Impressive entrance gate with a demarcated security cabin, intercom connectivity, and decorative compound wall enclosing the gated community.

🚗

Demarcated Stilt Parking

Covered ground stilt parking bays systematically allotted for Wings A, B, and C with wide internal driveway and driver/watchman facilities.

🛗

High-Speed Automatic Elevators

Modern, smooth elevators servicing all residential floors with battery backup to ensure uninterrupted vertical transit during power outages.

Solar Water Heating System

Rooftop solar water heating system providing energy-efficient hot water supply to reduce recurring household power bills.

Landscaped Garden & Sit-Outs

Invertor Ready Wiring

Quality Standards • Premium Materials

Architectural Specifications

Engineered with enduring materials, superior workmanship, and IS-compliant structural safeguards for multi-generational durability.

🏛️

R.C.C. Frame Structure

Earthquake-resistant reinforced cement concrete frame design adhering strictly to Bureau of Indian Standards (IS Code), engineered by Er. Bhavesh Patel.

🧱

Masonry & Plaster

High-quality brick masonry (6" thick external walls and 4" thick internal dividing walls). External double-coat sand-faced plaster and internal smooth gypsum / neeru finish.

✨

Vitrified Flooring

Premium 2' × 2' vitrified tile flooring across living, dining, and bedrooms with matching skirting. Anti-skid ceramic tiles in sit-outs, balconies, and dry terraces.

🍳

Granite Kitchen Platform

Polished granite kitchen counter with stainless steel sink (Nirali / equivalent). Full glazed ceramic tile dado up to lintel level with exhaust fan and water purifier provisions.

🚪

Doors & Hardware

Attractive laminated main entrance door with premium night latch and designer brass/SS hardware. Waterproof flush internal doors with cylindrical locks for all bedrooms.

🪟

Aluminum Sliding Windows

3-track powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh, polished green marble / granite window sills, and protective MS safety grills.

🚿

Bathrooms & Plumbing

Concealed CPVC plumbing with hot & cold water mixer unit. Glazed ceramic tiles dado up to 7' height. Premium CP fittings and elegant sanitary ware with geyser provision.

💡

Concealed Electrification

Fire-retardant concealed copper wiring (Polycab/Finolex) with modular switches (Legrand/Anchor). AC point in master bedroom, TV points in living, and MCBs on all circuits.

🎨

Painting & Finishes

Internal walls finished with smooth premium acrylic emulsion paint in pleasing shades. External facades coated with weather-shield exterior paint for enduring elegance.
