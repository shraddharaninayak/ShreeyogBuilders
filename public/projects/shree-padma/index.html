Shree Padma

Architectural Overview

Located directly opposite Cambridge School in prime Indira Nagar, Shree Padma features multi-wing 2 & 3 BHK premium residences across Wings A, B, and C with stilt parking, solar water heating, and manicured garden surroundings.

Conceived around enduring family relationships and peaceful community living, Shree Padma combines earthquake-resistant structural engineering, cross-ventilated unit layouts, and modern campus conveniences in Nashik.

Lifestyle Amenities

  • ✦ Grand Entrance Gate with Security Cabin & Intercom
  • ✦ Demarcated Stilt Covered Parking Bays for Wings A, B & C
  • ✦ High-Speed Automatic Elevators with Power Backup
  • ✦ Rooftop Solar Water Heating System
  • ✦ Paved Campus Perimeter with Senior Citizen Sit-Outs
  • ✦ Rainwater Harvesting System & Ground Water Recharge

Key Specifications

Structure

Earthquake-resistant R.C.C. frame structure designed as per IS standards.

Masonry & Plaster

Quality brick masonry (6" external & 4" internal) with external double-coat sand-faced plaster & internal gypsum/neeru finish.

Flooring

2' x 2' vitrified tile flooring in all rooms with 3" skirting; anti-skid ceramic tiles in sit-outs and balconies.

Kitchen

Polished granite kitchen platform with Nirali stainless steel sink and glazed tile dado up to lintel level.

Windows

3-track powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh, green marble/granite sills, and safety MS grills.

Electrification

Concealed copper wiring with modular switches, AC point in master bedroom, and TV points in living room.
