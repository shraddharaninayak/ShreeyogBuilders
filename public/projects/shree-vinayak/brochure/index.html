Shree Vinayak

"सुख-समृद्धीचा वास होऊ दे.. सर्व घराघरात !" • 2 & 3 BHK Premium Flats

Situated just 200 meters off the Mumbai-Agra National Highway behind Hotel Tapasvi in Indira Nagar, Shree Vinayak represents premium urban craftsmanship. Featuring 16 residences over a stilt parking level, the development offers spacious 3 BHK homes on the 1st floor, exclusive 2 BHK flats with personal 156 sq.ft private terraces on the 2nd floor, a Sanskar Mandir temple, high-speed elevator, and dedicated covered car parking for every homeowner.

Quality Engineering

Construction Specifications

Every element is selected to ensure lasting strength, aesthetic elegance, and low lifetime maintenance.

Structure & Walls

  • Earthquake-resistant RCC frame structure as per IS specifications
  • External 6" brick walls, internal 4" brick partitions
  • Double-coat sand-faced external plaster; neeru finish internal plaster

Flooring & Finishes

  • 2' x 2' vitrified tile flooring in all rooms
  • Anti-skid ceramic tile flooring in terraces and washrooms
  • Glazed wall tiles up to 7 ft height in all bathrooms

Kitchen

  • Black granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink
  • Glazed ceramic tiles up to lintel level above platform
  • Provision for exhaust fan and drinking water filter connection

Doors & Windows

  • Waterproof laminated main door with eye-hole & safety brass fittings
  • Waterproof flush doors for all bedrooms and bathrooms
  • 3-track powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with MS safety grill

Electrical

  • Concealed copper wiring with Anchor/Legrand modular switches
  • Adequate points in all rooms, TV & phone points in living room
  • Geyser point in bathroom & inverter wiring provision

Plumbing & Paint

  • Concealed plumbing with premium chrome-plated fittings
  • Solar water connection to master bathroom
  • Oil-bound distemper internally; weather-shield acrylic paint externally
