Architectural Overview

A Tradition of Excellence — Creating a Whole New Way of Comfort & Luxury Living. Located in prime Indira Nagar opposite LIC Colony, Shree Anant is an intimate G + 4 boutique landmark crafted with authentic red soil brick masonry and dual 9.0-meter road frontage.

Built by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers (Since 1992, CRISIL Rated), the project features zero common light bill solar net metering, an automatic 4-passenger elevator with battery backup, a dedicated rooftop party terrace with wash basin, and a comprehensive complimentary move-in fixture suite.

Key Specifications

Structure & Masonry

Earthquake resistant RCC frame with external 6" and internal 4" natural red soil brick walls.

Flooring & Tiling

Johnson / RAK premium vitrified tiles in all rooms; anti-skid ceramic in toilets & balconies.

Kitchen & Storage

L-shape or parallel black granite platform with SS sink, full-height dado & included modular trolleys.

Doors & Windows

Decorative main door with safety door; 3-track sliding windows with 4-side full granite frames.
