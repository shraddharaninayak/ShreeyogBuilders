Architectural Overview

"Peaceful living for infinity recognition of your divine soul.." Situated directly at Navshakti Chowk opposite the lush Dr. Homi Bhabha Garden in Bhabha Nagar, Shree Navashakti is an exclusive low-density residential development of 16 luxurious flats across G + 4 floors.

Built with authentic natural red soil bricks, rooftop solar net-metering (zero common light bills), solar water heating for each toilet, and a rooftop celebration terrace, Shree Navashakti blends structural permanence with serene family living.

Key Specifications

Structure

Earthquake-resistant RCC frame structure designed strictly as per IS standards.

Masonry

Natural red soil brick masonry providing superior climate insulation and cooler indoor ambient temperatures.

Solar Power

Rooftop solar generation system connected to net-metering for zero common electricity bills.

Kitchen

Polished granite platform with full tile dado, Nirali SS sink, and stainless steel modular kitchen trolley.

Doors & Windows

Box type plywood door frames with laminate on both sides; granite frames on all windows and toilets.

Finishes

Internal tractor emulsion plastic paint; external weather-shield plastic paint.
