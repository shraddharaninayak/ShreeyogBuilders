Shree Vrundavan

"एक आपुलकीचे घर" • Living Traditions in a Contemporary Way

Nestled in the tranquil, pollution-free atmosphere of Shreejay Nagar, Behind HDFC Bank, Indira Nagar, Nashik — just 1.5 kms from Mumbai-Agra Highway (NH-3) — Shree Vrundavan is a distinguished completed residential community by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers. Spread across two elegant wings ('A' Wing and 'B' Wing) over Stilt + 4 Floors, Shree Vrundavan offers well-ventilated 2 & 3 BHK premium homes featuring expansive private sit-outs, demarcated covered parking, dual automatic elevators, 24-hour municipal and borewell water supply, and complete Vastu compliance.

Comprehensive Technical Specifications

Every detail in Shree Vrundavan reflects stringent construction standards, earthquake-resistant design, and premium branded fittings for lifelong durability.

🏗️

R.C.C. Structure

Earthquake-resistant R.C.C. frame structure engineered to withstand seismic stresses according to national safety standards.

🧱

Brick Work & Masonry

External walls in 6" thick brick work for robust weather resistance and thermal insulation; internal partition walls in 4.5" thick brick masonry.

🎨

Plaster & Paint

Double-coat external plaster finished with premium semi-acrylic paint. Smooth Neeru-finish internal plaster with Asian Oil Bound Distemper paint.

✨

Flooring

Good quality 2' × 2' Vitrified (Johnson) tiles laid across the entire flat including living room, bedrooms, kitchen, and passages.

🍳

Kitchen

Granite kitchen platform (G20) with heavy-duty stainless steel sink and full-height glazed tile dado for effortless cleaning.

🪟

Windows

3-track powder-coated aluminum sliding windows complete with dedicated mosquito net, protective M.S. safety grills, and polished marble frames.

🚪

Doors & Frames

Main entrance door with brass fittings, night latch, and safety door. All internal doors and frames finished with premium laminate.

🚿

Bath & Toilet

Full-height glazed tiles in bathroom and toilet. PVC doors with polished marble frames, concealed plumbing with quality CP fittings and hot/cold wall mixer.

⚡

Electrification

Concealed copper electrification throughout the entire flat with modular switches and adequate power points.

📡

Cable & Telephone

Pre-wired telephone and television cable sockets strategically installed in the main living room.

🚗

Parking

Allotted covered parking space designated for each flat on the ground stilt floor.

💧

Water Supply

24-hour reliable water supply combining municipal tap connection and on-site deep borewell storage system.
