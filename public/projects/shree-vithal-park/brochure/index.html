Shree Vithal Park

"श्री ||विठ्ठल|| पार्क" • 2 & 3 BHK Luxurious Residences

Positioned along a prominent 30.00-meter wide DP thoroughfare near Charvak Chowk in Indira Nagar, Nashik — just 0.8 kms from Mumbai-Agra Highway (NH-3) — Shree Vithal Park is an accomplished residential landmark crafted by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers. Boasting an impressive 7-storey contemporary facade, Shree Vithal Park features generously proportioned 2 & 3 BHK luxurious flats with expansive private sit-outs, dedicated stilt covered car parking, automatic passenger elevator, integrated internal gas pipelines, 24-hour dual municipal and borewell water supply, and complete Vastu compliance.

Comprehensive Technical Specifications

Earthquake-resistant R.C.C. frame structure engineered to withstand seismic stresses according to national safety standards.

Brick Work & Masonry

Flooring

🍳

🪟

Windows

🚪

Doors & Frames

Gas Pipeline

Electrification

Water Supply
