Architectural Overview

Situated along Takali Road near Ashoka Marg and Bodhale Nagar in Nashik, Shree Nakshatra offers 16 exclusive 2 BHK apartments across G + 4 floors. Featuring private balconies, high-speed lift, 24-hour dual municipal/borewell water supply, pre-installed cable/telephone points, and allotted covered stilt parking.

Shree Nakshatra is designed for those seeking superior architectural design, resilient engineering, and luxury with economy. Offers spacious cross-ventilated suites, full Vastu compliance, and rapid transit access to both Mumbai-Agra NH-3 and Nashik-Pune Highway.

Key Specifications

Structure

Earthquake-resistant R.C.C. frame structure designed strictly as per IS standards.

Brick Work

External walls 6" thick and internal partition walls 4.5" thick brickwork.

Flooring

Good quality 2' x 2' Vitrified (Johnson) tile flooring for entire flat.

Kitchen

Granite L-Shape kitchen platform (G20) & stainless steel sink with full-height glazed tile dado.

Windows

3-track powder-coated aluminium sliding windows with Mosquito Net, M. S. safety grill & marble frame.

Plaster & Paint

Double-coat external plaster with semi-acrylic paint; Neeru-finish internal plaster with Asian Oil Bound Distemper paint.

Doors

Main door with brass fittings, Night latch and Safety door; All internal doors & frames with elegant laminate.

Bath / Toilet

Full-height designer glazed tiles in bathroom & toilet with concealed plumbing and wall mixer; PVC doors with marble frames.

Electrification

Concealed electrification for entire flat with modular switches and adequate light/fan points.

Water Supply

24 hours water supply with dual connections from Municipal mains and on-site borewell with storage tanks.

Parking

Allotted covered stilt car parking space on ground floor for each flat.

Media & Comms

Pre-installed television cable and telephone connection lines in living room.
