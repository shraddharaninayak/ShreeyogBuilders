Legacy • Integrity • Since 1992

Built with Natural Red Soil Bricks & Uncompromising Craftsmanship

For over three decades, Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers has been recognized for unshakeable structural integrity, transparent dealings, and on-time delivery across Nashik's finest neighbourhoods.

At Shree Anant, the pursuit of comfort and durability is reflected in every construction decision. Unlike ordinary modern high-rises built with hollow fly-ash blocks, Shree Anant employs authentic natural red soil bricks for internal and external masonry. This time-honoured technique guarantees superior thermal insulation, sound dampening, higher load-bearing resilience, and a naturally cooler living environment year-round.

As a CRISIL Rated Developer, Shreeyog adheres strictly to audited financial standards, statutory approvals, and transparent customer covenants. At Shree Anant, every homeowner receives complete peace of mind with MahaRERA registration (No. P51600022962), sanctioned NA permissions, and flawless completion standards.

With dual 9.0-meter road accessibility, every home is showered with unhindered natural daylight and fresh cross-ventilation, ensuring that each morning welcomes a breeze of vitality and serenity.

Precision Engineering • Included Fixtures

Comprehensive Technical Specifications

Every material and fitting selected for Shree Anant meets high construction standards, ensuring lasting beauty, safety, and acoustic comfort.

🏗️

Structure & Masonry

  • • R.C.C. framed structure designed strictly to withstand earthquake shocks as per IS codes.
  • • External 6" thick wall and internal 4" thick walls constructed in Natural Red Soil Bricks (or eco light blocks).
  • • External double coat sand face plaster for superior waterproofing.
  • • Internal walls finished with smooth gypsum / POP plaster ready for emulsion paint.
✨

Flooring & Wall Tiling

  • • Premium Johnson / RAK brand vitrified tiles (2' x 2') in living, dining, bedrooms & kitchen.
  • • Anti-skid ceramic tiles in all toilets, bathrooms, and attached dry balconies.
  • • Designer ceramic glazed wall tiles up to full ceiling height (dado) in all toilets.
  • • Glazed tile dado up to full lintel height above the kitchen platform.
🍳

Kitchen & Utility

  • • L-shape or parallel premium Black Granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink.
  • • Included modular kitchen trolley set with stainless steel baskets.
  • • Electrical and water inlet/drainage provisions for water purifier (Aqua Guard) and exhaust fan.
  • • Dedicated washing machine point and water drain in utility dry balcony.
🚪

Doors & Windows

  • • Decorative main entrance door with additional safety door, brass/SS hardware and eye piece.
  • • Box-type door frames with both sides laminated flush shutters.
  • • All windows and toilet frames with complete 4-side granite framing.
  • • 3-track powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh and MS safety grills.
🚿

Plumbing & Sanitary

  • • Concealed plumbing with heavy-duty CPVC/UPVC pipes and Jaguar or equivalent CP fittings.
  • • Premium sanitary ware (Cera / Johnson / Hindware) with dual flush cisterns.
  • • Hot and cold water wall mixer unit in all bathrooms with shower head.
  • • Direct solar hot water connection provided to the toilet.
⚡

Electrification & Power

  • • Concealed copper wiring (Polycab / Finolex) with MCBs and modular switches (Anchor Roma / Legrand).
  • • Adequate light, fan, and charging points; TV and telephone points in living & master bedroom.
  • • AC point provision in master bedroom; dedicated inverter backup wiring setup.
  • • Automatic 4-passenger elevator with battery backup system for uninterrupted movement.
