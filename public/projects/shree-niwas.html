Architectural Overview

Situated in prime Indira Nagar opposite LIC Colony, Shree Niwas offers 12 exclusive 2 & 3 BHK luxurious flats built with natural red soil bricks, rooftop solar net-metering, campus play garden, and a private celebration terrace.

A boutique G + 4 residential landmark of only 12 residences with 3 homes per floor, featuring authentic red soil brick masonry, zero common light bills via net solar power, an automatic 4-person elevator with battery backup, and complete move-in complimentary furnishings.
