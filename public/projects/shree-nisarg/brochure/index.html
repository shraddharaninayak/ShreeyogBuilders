Shree Nisarg

"Relax in the soothing lap of mother nature" • 2 & 3 BHK Luxurious Flats

Situated in the prestigious cultural and educational heartland of Gangapur Road opposite Pramod Nagar, Shree Nisarg harmonizes urban luxury with serene botanical greens. Featuring twin wings (A & B) rising over a stilt parking level with a dedicated landscaped roadside front park, this boutique community offers 24 residences: 3 BHK homes (1,270 sq.ft) in Wing A and 2 BHK homes (1,025 sq.ft) in Wing B, dual elevators, and dedicated covered car bays.

Nature-Centric Philosophy

Where Greenery Meets Sophistication

True to its name 'Nisarg' (Nature), the project is crafted around botanical landscaping, generous cross-breezes, and Gangapur Road's pleasant micro-climate.

01

Landscaped Front Park

A lush green public-facing garden along the 12-meter road creates a green buffer and welcoming entrance experience.

02

Distinct Wings A & B

Wing A is exclusively dedicated to spacious 3 BHK residences (1,270 sq.ft) while Wing B hosts comfortable 2 BHK homes (1,025 sq.ft).

03

Independent Lifts

Two distinct automatic elevators (one per wing) guarantee swift vertical movement and total resident privacy.

04

Elite Address

Positioned opposite Pramod Nagar near Nirmala Convent, surrounded by Nashik's finest schools, cafes, and verdant avenues.

Thoughtful Amenities

Enriching Amenities Surrounded by Greenery

Equipped with reliable infrastructure and natural community areas that make daily life delightful.

Roadside Landscaped Park

Lush front landscaped garden with ornamental trees, flowering shrubs, lawn mounds, and decorative entrance pavers.

Dual High-Speed Lifts

Two independent passenger elevators (one in Wing A, one in Wing B) with battery backup and emergency descent.

24 Demarcated Stilt Bays

Covered ground stilt parking with 24 numbered parking bays (A-1 to A-8 and B-1 to B-16) and wide driveway.

Security & Boundary

Aesthetic perimeter compound wall, designer entrance gate, security cabin, and intercom connectivity.

Dual Water Supply

Municipal water connection coupled with on-site borewell, underground sump, and overhead tanks for uninterrupted water.

Rainwater Recharge

Eco-conscious rainwater harvesting setup recharging groundwater tables and conserving resources.

Quality Engineering

Construction Specifications

Engineered to high Indian Standard specifications, using top-tier structural materials, fittings, and refined interior finishes.

Structure & Walls

  • Earthquake-resistant RCC frame structure engineered to high safety standards
  • 6" thick external and internal brick masonry
  • Double-coat sand-faced plaster externally; smooth gypsum/neeru finish internally

Flooring & Tiling

  • Premium 2' x 2' vitrified tile flooring in drawing, dining, kitchen, and bedrooms
  • Anti-skid ceramic flooring in attached terraces and washrooms
  • Designer glazed full-height dado tiles in all bathrooms

Kitchen

  • Fine-polished black granite counter with stainless steel sink
  • Designer glazed tiles up to lintel level above platform
  • Adequate electric points for exhaust fan, microwave, and water purifier

Doors & Windows

  • Teak wood frame ornamental main entrance door with brass fittings
  • Waterproof flush doors for bedrooms and bathrooms
  • Powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh & safety MS grills

Electrification

  • Concealed copper wiring with Anchor/Legrand modular switches
  • AC point in master bedroom; TV and telephone connections in living room
  • Inverter wiring provision for uninterrupted illumination

Plumbing & Paint

  • Concealed plumbing with Jaguar or equivalent premium CP fittings
  • Solar water heating connection in master bathroom
  • Superior acrylic washable interior paint; durable weather-shield exterior coating
