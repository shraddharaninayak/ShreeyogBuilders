Architectural Overview

A Classic Style of Life — Better Environment Invites You For Your Better Tomorrow. Positioned in prime Indira Nagar opposite LIC Colony, Shree Niwas is an exclusive residential enclave of just 12 homes across G + 4 floors, with only 3 residences per floor.

Built by Shreeyog Builders & Land Developers (Since 1992, CRISIL Rated), the project features genuine red soil brick construction, zero common light bill solar net metering, an automatic 4-person elevator with battery backup, a landscaped children's play area with green gym, and a rooftop celebration terrace.

Lifestyle Amenities

  • ✦ Natural Red Soil Brick Construction for Enduring Durability & Thermal Comfort
  • ✦ Children's Play Area, Landscaped Lawn & Open Green Gym on Ground Level
  • ✦ Rooftop Solar System for Common Utility Net-Metering (Zero Common Light Bill)
  • ✦ Rooftop Celebration Terrace with Built-In Wash Basin & Water Facility
  • ✦ Automatic 4-Person Elevator with Emergency Battery Backup (ARD)
  • ✦ Solar Hot Water Supply Directly Connected to Toilets in Every Home
  • ✦ Demarcated Covered Car Parking Bays with Proposed 9.0-Meter Road Access
  • ✦ 24/7 CCTV Video Surveillance & Security Watchman Room with Toilet
  • ✦ Turnkey Living Package (Tube Lights, Bulbs, Fans, Curtains, Aqua Guard, Accessories)
